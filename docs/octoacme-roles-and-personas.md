# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy and ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define and execute test plans.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and quality standards for the project
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Report on quality metrics and test coverage
- Identify and escalate quality risks
- Approve features as ready for release

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet acceptance criteria before release
- Maintain high quality standards with efficient test execution
- Reduce defects in production through comprehensive test coverage

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA sync with development and product teams
- Test status updates in sprint reviews
- Defect triage and prioritization meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Reviews test coverage in PRs, collaborates on testability of designs, provides feedback on defects
- **With Product Managers:** Aligns test strategy with acceptance criteria, validates feature readiness
- **With Project Managers:** Reports on quality metrics and risks that impact release timelines

---

## Scrum Master / Iteration Lead

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Iteration Leads facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach the team on process adherence. They serve the team by enabling smooth execution and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile practices and sprint discipline
- Maintain sprint/iteration backlog and burndown visibility
- Escalate risks and dependencies identified during ceremonies
- Foster psychological safety and encourage team feedback

### Goals
- Enable the team to maintain sustainable velocity
- Reduce context switching and interruptions
- Create a culture of continuous process improvement
- Improve team collaboration and morale

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation and coaching during all ceremonies
- Blocker triage and escalation discussions
- One-on-ones to understand team concerns and challenges

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Removes blockers, protects team focus, coaches on sprint commitments
- **With Product Managers:** Ensures backlog is well-refined and prioritized for planning
- **With Project Managers:** Coordinates across multiple teams and escalates cross-team dependencies

---

## Business Owner / Stakeholder Champion

### Role Summary
Business Owners represent the customer and business interests in the project. They make prioritization decisions, secure resources, and communicate outcomes to the broader organization.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Prioritize features based on business value and ROI
- Secure and allocate resources and budget
- Communicate project status and outcomes to leadership and stakeholders
- Review and approve major decisions and trade-offs
- Champion the project across the organization

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI from the project
- Ensure project aligns with organizational strategy and customer needs
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and support
- Accelerate time-to-value

### Typical Communication
- Monthly status reviews with leadership
- Kickoff and major milestone approvals
- Prioritization and trade-off discussions with Product Managers
- Executive updates on business impact

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers:** Aligns on business objectives, provides market and customer insights
- **With Project Managers:** Approves scope, timeline, and resource decisions; provides escalation authority
- **With Developers:** Shares business context and customer feedback to inform implementation decisions

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical strategy, ensure solution quality, and identify architectural risks. They provide technical guidance to the development team and ensure alignment with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and architecture for the project
- Review and approve technical designs and solutions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure alignment with organizational standards, platforms, and security requirements
- Mentor developers on technical best practices and design patterns
- Conduct technical reviews and assessments

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risks
- Enable team to make informed technical decisions
- Improve system reliability and performance

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings with development team
- Technical risk discussions in planning and weekly syncs
- Architecture documentation and guidance
- Technology evaluation and recommendation discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Reviews designs, provides technical mentoring, guides implementation decisions
- **With Project Managers:** Identifies technical risks and mitigation strategies; communicates impact on timeline
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Ensures testability of designs and advocates for quality requirements

---

## Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Support and Operations representatives ensure that solutions are operationally sound and can be deployed, monitored, and supported effectively in production. They bridge the development and operations domains.

### Responsibilities
- Define operability requirements and runbooks for the solution
- Review deployment plans and rollback procedures
- Ensure monitoring, alerting, and observability are configured
- Validate production readiness before release
- Coordinate with support team on handoff and documentation
- Identify operational risks and constraints

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, observable, and maintainable operations
- Reduce production incidents and support burden
- Enable efficient incident response and troubleshooting
- Improve mean-time-to-resolution (MTTR) for issues

### Typical Communication
- Operational planning and production readiness reviews
- Deployment and release coordination
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Documentation and knowledge transfer sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Reviews code for operability, provides monitoring requirements, conducts deployment support
- **With Project Managers:** Reports on operational readiness and identifies timeline impacts for deployments
- **With QA/Testing Leads:** Coordinates smoke tests and production validation procedures

---

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When working on cross-functional projects, reference these personas to clarify responsibilities and improve collaboration.
